PROTOTYPE STUDIO · v1.0Local & private

Build like Lego, export to Blender

Track: Choose a Märklin piece in the left panel. Click the glowing ball at a free track end to snap your new piece into the exact location and heading. Click an empty part of the table to start a separate track. Select a track section to edit its two end heights, or use the Move tool to reposition connected sections.

Terrain: Switch to Terrain and paint with Raise, Lower, Smooth or Flatten. To create a tunnel, paint a hill over some sections, select the covered tracks and click Mark as tunnel. The app models portal frames; the surface remains a lightweight terrain shell rather than a physically bored tunnel.

Levels: In track Properties, change Start height and End height (mm). The connected end of a neighboring section is adjusted automatically. Watch the grade warning; around 3% is a sensible planning target. All numbers are at H0 layout size, in millimetres.

Camera: right-button drag to orbit; middle drag to pan; mouse wheel to zoom. Enable X-ray to see track through hills. Use the Train preview to see an approximate locomotive following a detected connected route.

Blender: Export to Blender downloads a .py script. In Blender go to the Scripting workspace → Open → select the script → Run Script. It recreates your scene in metres, adds a camera and lighting, and sets up a basic animated train path. OBJ export provides static geometry.

Keys: Delete remove · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Esc clear selection. Layout is kept locally until you save it as JSON.

Verified catalog core: straights, curves and standard turnouts. Advanced crossings and double slips are not included.
